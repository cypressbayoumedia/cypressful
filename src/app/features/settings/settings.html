Contentful Integration

Cypressful requires a Personal Access Token (CMA Token) to manage your content.

How to get your token:

  1. Log in to your Contentful Dashboard.
  2. Click Settings in the top navigation.
  3. Select CMA Tokens under Space Settings.
  4. Click Create personal access token.
  5. Give it a name (e.g., "Cypressful App") and click Generate.
  6. Copy the token and paste it above.
